      Confirmed

      1. Roof 16.5 × 12.4 m, 1.5 m east overhang, raised 200 mm (rev 2026-11-25f).
      2. Kitchen counters 900 mm above the finished inside floor (all kitchen counters).
      3. Typical window tops 2 300 mm above FFL. Kitchen window 15 is 900 × 1 100, sill 1 200, head 2 300, rail top 1 185 (rev h; the 2 360 on B-15 rev g was wrong). The high window is 970 × 1 190, sill 2 600, head 3 790, a factory unit with two top-hung sashes, hinges and a winder.
      4. Structural timber is Douglas fir, C24 or equivalent. The central beam may be C24-graded pine at the same section; no round or ungraded poles (rev g item 12).
      5. House, utility N/S/W and kitchen under-sill walls are a sandwich: 150 mm timber frame with insulation and up to 50 mm cob/plaster skin each face (250 mm total). All outside walls have the same lime render plus limewash finish and colour, including the utility E wall and the N/S/W returns, so the house reads as one colour with no visible change at the corners (rev j). The skin thins or stops at the posts and knee braces; the timber stays exposed or lime-washed (Roundhouse Control, 25 Nov 2026). The utility east (installation) wall is the same outer face line at CL+125: 150 mm frame, 22 mm ply inside (face at CL−97) with timber stop beads at the two inside corners, and outside 25 × 8 laths at 40 c/c, then a cob/clay skin and lime render, 50 mm including the laths (rev i). No boards on battens.
      6. Kitchen window 15: timber rail top 1 185 mm (85 mm above the 1 100 shelf), 15 mm fit gap, then the window from 1 200 to 2 300 (rev h).
      7. The 15 mm gap between the 15 mm packer (top 385 mm) and the 400 mm window sill is an intended fit gap, filled with shims and sill tape — not a mismatch.
